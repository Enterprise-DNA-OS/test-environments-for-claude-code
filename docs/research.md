Checked 31 October 2026. Family: test-environment-coordination, absent from the registry at selection. Existing software-testing holds test cases; software-service-ownership holds service ownership. This desk coordinates shared environment instances, reservation contention, version requirements and evidence-led handovers.

## Annual bill

US$96,000 a year is the illustrative licence cost for 200 instances in the March 2025 [Bloor study hosted by Enov8](https://www.enov8.com/wp-content/uploads/00002915-Enov8-Environment-Management-InBrief-Mar-25.pdf). Its example has 250 development/testing people and excludes implementation and training. It is not a current quote, a small-business invoice, or a verified Enterprise DNA customer bill.

The [current price-overview URL](https://www.enov8.com/priceoverview/) redirects to contact sales. There is no current public list-price figure to put on the landing page, so prices is empty and the copy asks for the owner's actual quote.

## Selection judgment

Scores: annual bill 4, data-shaped 5, our buyers 1, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 4. Total 20/30. The high bill is credible as an enterprise example, but applicability to a 10-to-50-person owner-led buyer is weak. A scan of available local customer files and CRM snapshots found no named Enov8 customer. No live CRM read was made and no customer demand is claimed.

Compared AppFollow app-review operations: 1/5/1/2/5/5 = 19/30. Its public [Team pricing](https://appfollow.io/pricing) did not establish the required five-figure annual bill, so it failed the first filter. Compared LaunchNotes release communications: 1/5/1/2/4/4 = 17/30. [Published Growth pricing](https://www.launchnotes.com/pricing) also failed that filter; Premium is quoted. These are prioritisation judgments, not measured market scores.

Search results contain Enov8 pricing and alternative lists, including [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/enov8-environment-management/competitors/alternatives). Search volume and ranking difficulty were not measured. The list mixes testing and environment tools and is not used to assert true rivals.

The build is a local coordination desk, not the full Enov8 suite. It does not discover infrastructure, run live health checks, deploy code, provision or mask data, or recreate vendor diagrams. The sample CSV and demo records are synthetic. Captured page outputs come from actual demo command runs. No claim is made that the vendor cannot reproduce the analysis.
